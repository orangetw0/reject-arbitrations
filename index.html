Before you mail it

  1. Print the PDF and compare every account number with your statements.
  2. Sign above your printed name in ink. A typed name is not a personal signature.
  3. Keep a copy or photo of the signed letter.
  4. Address the envelope exactly as shown and mail it early. The deadline is the day Capital One receives it, not the postmark date.
  5. For proof of delivery, ask the post office for Certified Mail with a return receipt and keep the tracking number.

Per Capital One's notice, the rejection covers existing accounts only, not accounts you open later, and does not change your other rights or responsibilities.
